Doors

Flush and sliding doors, made to fit.

We make the doors too: solid core laminated doors and their frames, built to the opening on site and finished to match your carpentry.

Doors
01 A flat door face with the frame hidden, so the door sits in line with the wall and the joins almost disappear. For bedrooms, studies and storerooms you want to blend in. Ask about flush doors Flush doorsConcealed frame Doors
02 Doors that run on a track instead of swinging out, to save floor space. Slim frames with mirror, glass or panel inserts. Ask about sliding doors SlidingDoors Doors
03 A solid core door is heavier and quieter than a hollow one, and it holds its shape. We laminate both faces in the finish you choose, so it matches your wardrobes and walls. Ask about solid core doors Solid coreLaminated doors Doors
04 We make the frames as well, so the door, frame and finish come from one workshop and fit together. Concealed or visible, built to your opening. Ask about door frames DoorFrames
