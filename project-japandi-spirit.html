Japandi Spirit

A sleek, minimalistic, wabi-sabi interior of Scandinavian and Japanese design. This home is a visual storyboard of consciously layered earthy colours and natural materials, without forgoing functionality.

25 Rosewood Drive · 2022
(Residential · HDB)

The communal space works equally well for one person or a group of up to ten. The client loves to host friends and family, so a built-in settee was cleverly designed into the dining zone.

Light oak, soft linen and warm white paint do the heavy lifting. Every surface was chosen to age gracefully and to let the natural light from the full-width windows carry the room.
