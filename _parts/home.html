FAQ

Questions.

Anything else, just ask us.

Contact us
Do you make doors?

Yes. We make solid core laminated doors and door frames, flush doors with concealed frames, and sliding doors, all built to your opening and finished to match your carpentry. Send us the opening size and we'll quote after we measure on site.

How long does a made-to-order piece take?

Most furniture is ready in 4–6 weeks from your order. Bespoke carpentry is scheduled after the drawing is approved, usually 3–5 weeks of fabrication before installation.

Can I change the size of a piece in the shop?

Yes. Every piece has an "Ask about a size" link. Small changes are often the same price; we'll confirm before you pay.

Is delivery included?

Delivery and placement are free within mainland Singapore for orders above $1,500. Wall-mounted pieces include installation.

Do you work with my own interior designer?

Yes. We fabricate to drawings from homeowners, designers and contractors. Send us your shop drawings for a quote.

What warranty comes with the work?

Carpentry and furniture carry a one-year workmanship warranty covering hinges, runners and joinery.
