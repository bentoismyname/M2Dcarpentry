About M2D

One team, two generations.

M2D Carpentry is the renovation and carpentry arm of M2D. M2 Decor Pte Ltd was established as a one-stop, streamlined interior design and renovation company in Singapore, with a foundation deeply rooted in craftsmanship.

Since 2001

The birth of craftsmanship.

  1. 2001Where it all startedM2D's story begins at a carpentry bench. Benjamin's father built the business on woodwork: cabinets, built-ins and the quiet discipline of making things that last.
  2. Father and sonTwo generations in one workshopThe first ran the fabrication and the crews. The second grew up on site, learned the trade from the ground up and brought design into the conversation.
  3. 2015Changing homes, changing livesSince 2015 we have focused on Singapore residential design and renovation, treating each home like a consultation rather than a sale.
  4. TodayOne team, four divisionsInterior Design and Consultancy, Renovation and Carpentry, the Interior Design Workshop, and Handyman and Home Repair. Different colours, one standard of work.

How we work

Workshop, site, handover.

We do not believe in a showroom. Our carpentry is fabricated in our own workshop and installed by our own crew.

Our story

We do more than furniture.

M2D Carpentry grew up building bespoke interior fit-outs for hotels, restaurants and offices. Today most of our work is residential: wardrobes, kitchens, feature walls, doors and built-ins made to fit the way you live.

A second-generation Singaporean workshop that builds everything in-house, so we can give you better costing and keep the quality in our own hands.
