Where it All started

2001

It began at a carpentry bench

M2D's story begins at a carpentry bench. Benjamin's father built the business on woodwork: cabinets, built-ins and the quiet discipline of making things that last. Rooted in the artistry of crafting wood, that background still shapes how we take on every project today.

Father and son

Two generations in one workshop

The first ran the fabrication and the crews; the second grew up on site, learned the trade from the ground up and brought design into the conversation. The company name honours that lineage: M Square Decor, the renovation and carpentry arm, and M2D Interior, the design studio.

2015

Into Singapore homes

Since 2015 we have focused on Singapore residential design and renovation, treating each home like a consultation rather than a sale. Over 200 households later, we are a trusted design-and-build firm with in-house carpentry, M&E and masonry, and a CaseTrust, HDB and BCA accredited team.

Today

M2D Carpentry

M2D has grown into four divisions under one roof. M2D Carpentry is the Renovation and Carpentry division: our carpentry is fabricated in our own workshop and installed by our own crew.

The process

From conception to execution.

Tell us the size and the carpenter who will build it will draw it up.

01

Tell us about the space

Photos, a floor plan or rough measurements are enough to start.

02

Site measure and drawing

We measure on site and send shop drawings with materials and a fixed quote.

03

Fabrication

Every piece is cut, edged, assembled and finished by our own team, in our own workshop. Bespoke carpentry is usually 3–5 weeks of fabrication once the drawing is approved.

04

Installation

Installed by the people who built it. Carpentry and furniture carry a one-year workmanship warranty covering hinges, runners and joinery.
